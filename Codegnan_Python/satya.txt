Once upon a time, a hungry jackal ran away from a noisy pack of dogs. 
In his rush, he tripped and fell right into a big tub of bright blue dye.
When he climbed out, he was completely blue from head to tail.
He walked back into the forest, and the other animals stared at him in fear and wonder.
 The blue jackal puffed out his chest and said, "The king of the gods sent me to rule over all of you!
"The animals believed him and began to serve him. 
The blue jackal felt very proud and important.
One evening, a group of real jackals in the distance let out a loud howl. 
Hearing them, the blue jackal forgot where he was and started howling back just like a normal jackal.
The other animals realized he was just a regular jackal playing a trick, and they chased him out of the forest.
